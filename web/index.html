    Meet Zen
    
    
    Zen is a synth: a neural network that begins as random numbers, knowing no words at all.
      It lives in a little house in a quiet hollow with Wren, who raises it.
    
    
    
      
    🎓 Zen studiesThe Academy trains its brain from scratch on your GPU: web text streamed from FineWeb, Wren's lessons and its own life. Watch it go from static to babble to first words and sentences.
    
      
    🪶 Wren raises itWren is a language model that already knows language. It cooks, gardens, keeps the fire going, names things for Zen, talks with it turn by turn and tells it a bedtime story every night.
    
      
    🏡 A deep little worldRooms warm by the fire and cool at night. Soup cooks, bread bakes, food goes stale, rain soaks things, plants need water, hens lay eggs, Moss the cat finds the warmest spot.
    
      
    🗣️ Words become deedsWhen Zen's brain can write "I feed the cat.", it feeds the cat. Until then its needs and Wren decide, and it studies how the words fit what happens.

      •
