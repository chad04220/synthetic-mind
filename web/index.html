    Meet it
    
    
    A small language model lives in this world. It sees what is around it, decides, and acts: it walks, digs, builds, crafts, and talks. Each decision takes a fraction of a second on your GPU.
    
    
    
      
    👀 WatchDrag to look around, wheel to zoom. The mind card shows what it thinks and does right now.
    
      
    💬 TalkType in the chat. It answers in its own words, and listens while it works.
    
      
    🧍 VisitWalk in with WASD and the mouse. Hold left-click to dig, right-click to place or use, 1–9 to choose, E inventory, G give it what you hold.
    
      
    👍 TeachRate its decisions on the Mind tab, or show it what it should have done. Practice turns that into a better model.
