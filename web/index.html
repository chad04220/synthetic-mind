    Meet Zen
    
    
    Zen is a synth: a neural network that begins as random numbers, knowing no words at all.
      It lives alone in an endless, wild world, with only a small glowing Sprite for company.
    
    
    
      
    🎓 Zen studiesThe Academy trains its brain from scratch on your GPU: web text streamed from FineWeb and its own life. Watch it go from static to babble to first words and sentences.
    
      
    🦾 Every deed is its ownEach step, turn, bite and word is chosen by its brain. Nothing else moves it. It learns from what happens: warmth, food, water, new places, pain.
    
      
    🌍 An endless worldMeadows, forests, deserts, mountains, rivers and lakes, ruins and caves, deer, foxes and owls; days, nights, seasons and weather. The Atlas maps what it explores.
    
      
    🌟 The Sprite of All KnowingWhen Zen asks, the Sprite answers. When Zen paints, sings or sculpts, the Sprite looks or listens and tells it what it sees.
    
    
    
    
    You can
    
    
      
      
    • 👀 Watch: drag to look around, click Zen, the Sprite or anything to look closer. 🕊️ Free flies over the land.
      • 
      
    • 🧍 Visit: walk with WASD and the mouse; E picks up, G gives Zen what you carry, T talks.
      • 
      
    • 💬 Talk: to Zen (it answers with its own brain) or ask the Sprite on its tab.
      • 
      
    • ♥ Love the keepsakes you like: it teaches Zen your taste.
      • 
    
    
    
    The first time, the Sprite's model (about 3 GB) is downloaded and started on your CPU; the Sprite tab shows the progress. Your GPU is kept for training.
