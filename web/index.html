    Meet Zen
    
    
    Zen is a synth: a neural network that begins as random numbers, knowing no words at all.
      It learns language by studying, and by living in a valley full of other minds.
    
    
    
      
    🎓 It studiesThe Academy trains its brain on your GPU from web text, lessons and its own life. Watch it go from static to babble to first words and sentences.
    
      
    🌍 It livesIts own words steer its body: when it can say "I go fishing", it goes fishing. Until then its needs decide, and it learns from that.
    
      
    🧑‍🤝‍🧑 It has neighboursSeven villagers, each a mind with its own language model, needs, thoughts and friendships. They talk with each other and with Zen, one turn at a time.
    
      
    🎨 They createPaintings, songs you can play, sculptures, buildings and poems: all kept in the Gallery for good.
    
    
    
    
    You can
    
    
      
      
    • 👀 Watch: drag to look around, click anyone to see their mind.
      • 
      
    • 🧍 Visit: walk the valley with WASD and the mouse; E talks or picks up, G gives Zen what you carry, H hugs.
      • 
      
    • 💬 Talk: say anything to Zen or to the villager you face. The Oracle suggests the reply Zen should learn.
      • 
      
    • 🧺 Add things: place apples, books with your own words, materials and tools.
      • 
    
    
    
    The first time, the Oracle's and the villagers' voices are downloaded (about 3 GB) and started on your CPU; the Oracle tab shows the progress. Your GPU is kept for training.
